ZenFlow helps you slow down, breathe and focus, with a small daily practice that fits your day.

BREATHE
Follow guided breathing exercises (Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Calming, Energizing and Coherent breathing) with a
soft animated guide, gentle haptics and optional bowl chimes.

FOCUS
Use the Pomodoro timer with work and break cycles, a distraction-free deep focus mode and clear progress.

RELAX
Choose calming ambient sounds such as ocean waves, rain, forest and white noise, with a sleep timer that fades
the sound out for you.

GROW
Watch your Zen tree grow with every session. Keep a streak with a built-in rest day, check in with your mood,
and see your week at a glance.

MADE TO FIT YOUR DAY
- Daily reminder you control, and widget and launcher shortcuts
- Light, dark and optional dynamic colors
- English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French and Brazilian Portuguese
- Large text, TalkBack and reduced-motion support; adapts to tablets and foldables

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. Your sessions and mood check-ins stay on your device.

ZenFlow is a relaxation and mindfulness tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or
prevent any condition. If you feel unwell or have a breathing condition, talk to a health professional and
