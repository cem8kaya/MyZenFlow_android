# Audio licenses

All audio in this folder is synthesized by `tools/audio/generate_audio.py` from noise,
filters and additive synthesis. No recording, sample or third-party asset is used.

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| ambient_ocean.ogg, ambient_rain.ogg, ambient_forest.ogg, ambient_white_noise.ogg | Seamless 32 s loops |
| cue_bowl_start.ogg, cue_bowl_end.ogg, cue_chime_phase.ogg | Singing-bowl and chime cues |

License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public-domain dedication). No attribution required.

Regenerate with `python3 tools/audio/generate_audio.py` (needs numpy and ffmpeg with libvorbis).

If you replace these with recorded CC0 audio (for example from Freesound or OpenGameArt), list
each file here with its source URL, author and license, and keep a copy of the license text.
Do not use CC-BY-NC or CC-BY-SA material: Google Play distribution with in-app purchases needs
commercial-use-compatible licenses, and CC-BY requires visible attribution in the app.
