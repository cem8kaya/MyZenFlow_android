## What the app stores
All of the following stays **on your device** and is never sent to us:
- your optional name and preferences (language, theme, reminders, sound and haptics)
- your practice history (breathing, focus and meditation sessions), streaks and achievements
- your optional mood check-ins

We do not run user accounts, show ads, or use analytics or advertising identifiers.

## Backups
Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account (Auto Backup) and restore it on a new device.
This is a system feature controlled by your device settings. We do not receive this data.

## Purchases
If you buy ZenFlow Premium, the payment is processed by **Google Play**. We do not see or store your payment
details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Premium. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchase data.

## Notifications and permissions
- Notifications (optional): the daily reminder and timer alerts. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.
- Vibration: haptic feedback during breathing exercises (optional).
- Wake lock / foreground service: keeps the focus timer running when the screen is off.

## Children
ZenFlow is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app or use Android Settings > Apps > ZenFlow > Storage > Clear storage. Because the data is only on your device, we cannot access or delete it for you.

## Health disclaimer
ZenFlow is a relaxation and mindfulness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

## Changes
We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data and change the date above.
